The SmallRig CT195 is the FreeBlazer Aluminum Alloy Video Tripod features reverse-folding 4-section aluminum alloy legs with knob locks for quick height adjustments up to 185cm (about 6 feet). The detachable center column reverses for low-angle shots and converts one leg into a monopod setup. It includes a one-click rotating Arca-type QR plate for fast horizontal-to-vertical switching, a telescopic ambidextrous pan handle, 360° panning, and ±90° tilting.
