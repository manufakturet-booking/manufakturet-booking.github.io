The Stairville DR-1 is a versatile, 1U 19-inch rack-mountable DMX-512 recorder and playback device designed to capture complex light shows in real time. Users can record up to 12 standalone shows directly onto an SD card from any standard DMX console, allowing for easy live playback with variable speed settings, loop functions, and internal clock scheduling.
